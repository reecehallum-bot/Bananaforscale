How we measure bananas

Lengths are measured along the outer curve from blossom end to tip — the USDA standard for grading.

  • Cavendish — 17.78 cm (7 in): USDA medium grade. The world's most-consumed banana and the de facto "banana for scale" internet standard.
  • Lady Finger — 11.4 cm (4.5 in): Midpoint of the typical 10–13 cm retail range for Musa acuminata 'Lady Finger'.
  • Plantain — 25.4 cm (10 in): Conservative midpoint of the 20–30 cm grocery-store range for cooking plantains.
  • Red Banana — 15.2 cm (6 in): Typical retail length for Musa acuminata 'Red Dacca'.

Sources: USDA AMS · Specialty Produce · Produce Market Guide
